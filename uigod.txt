1. MATHEMATICAL SANS-SERIF BOLD CONVERTER (bold() Helper)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Instead of raw standard <b>text</b> tags or basic markdown, autoShopify converts
  ASCII text dynamically into Unicode Mathematical Sans-Serif Bold characters:
    Uppercase: A-Z -> 𝗔-𝗭 (U+1D5D4 to U+1D5ED)
    Lowercase: a-z -> 𝗮-𝘇 (U+1D5EE to U+1D607)
    Digits:    0-9 -> 𝟬-𝟵 (U+1D7EC to U+1D7F5)
- Example output:
    bold("Single Check") -> 𝗦𝗶𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗲 𝗖𝗵𝗲𝗰𝗸
    bold("Checked by:") -> 𝗖𝗵𝗲𝗰𝗸𝗲𝗱 𝗯𝘆:
